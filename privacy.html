PRIVACY POLICY.

Samantha Used Car ("we") operates samanthausedcar.com. This policy explains what personal information we process, why, and your rights — as required by the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보보호법). 한국어 전문은 아래에 있습니다.

1. What we collect & why

DataRequired?Purpose
Chat messages you type to the AI assistantRequired to use the chatGenerating answers, matching you with vehicles
Name, preferred visit timeOnly if you submit the contact formConnecting you with Samantha for a viewing/consultation
Phone numberOptionalDirect call/text follow-up you request
Country/city of access, page views, car-page clicksAutomatic (no raw IP stored by us)Understanding visits and popular cars

2. AI chatbot notice

Messages you send to the chat are transmitted to our AI infrastructure providers solely to generate a response and to record the conversation for customer-service review. We do not use your inputs to train AI models.

3. Retention

Chat records and leads: up to 1 year, then deleted; earlier upon your request. Aggregated statistics (counts by day/country) contain no personal identifiers and may be kept longer.

4. Third-party provision

We do not provide your personal information to third parties for their own use. (제3자 제공: 없음)

5. Processors (처리 위탁)

  • Vercel Inc. — website/API hosting, request handling
  • Upstash — storage of chat/lead records and aggregate counters
  • Requesty & underlying AI model providers — chat response generation
  • Google (Analytics) — traffic analytics
  • Meta Platforms (Pixel) — ad performance measurement
  • GitHub Pages — static site hosting (no personal data processed)

6. Destruction

Electronic records are permanently deleted from our storage when the retention period ends or upon verified request.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information at any time: email flowerdudtlr@gmail.com or WhatsApp +82-10-7170-4513. We respond within 10 days.

8. Security

HTTPS everywhere; data access limited to authorized accounts (Google-authenticated allowlist); minimal collection by design; no raw IP addresses stored in our own records.
