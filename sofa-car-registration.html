What is SOFA vehicle registration in Korea?

US military members and other SOFA-status personnel in Korea register their privately owned vehicles under SOFA rules — different from a regular Korean registration — and the vehicle must also be registered for base access. The paperwork is in Korean and has several steps, which is why most buyers near Camp Humphreys have a specialist handle it.
